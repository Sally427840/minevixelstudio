STEP 1 OF 5 👋
Welcome to Minevixel Studio!
The world's first AI powered Minecraft Bedrock addon creator. Let's take a quick tour so you know exactly how to use it.

✨ You can create custom mobs, blocks and items just by describing them or uploading a photo — no coding needed!

STEP 2 OF 5 📸
Step 1 — Upload or Describe
Take a photo of anything — an animal, drawing, object — or just type a description of what you want to create.

💡 Tip: The more detail you add to your description the better your addon will be! Try something like "A glowing blue wolf with fire abilities that follows the player"

STEP 3 OF 5 🤖
Step 2 — AI Generates Your Addon
Our AI reads your photo and description and builds a complete Minecraft Bedrock addon — model, textures, behaviors and all.

⚡ Generation takes about 10-15 seconds. You will see a live 3D preview building during this time!

STEP 4 OF 5 ⬇️
Step 3 — Install to Minecraft
Click Install to Minecraft to download your .mcaddon file. Then open it on your device and Minecraft will install it automatically!

🎮 To spawn your mob in game type: /summon minevixel:your_addon_name

STEP 5 OF 5 🌍
Share With the Community!
